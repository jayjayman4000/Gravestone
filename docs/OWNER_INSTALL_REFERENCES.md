## How to read the status

- **CONDITIONAL PERMISSION:** retained evidence identifies a download/use basis, subject to its stated conditions and your jurisdiction. Check current publisher terms before downloading, storing, extracting search text or transferring between your own devices. Permission for one action does not establish permission for the others.
- **CHECK TERMS:** unresolved permission; this is **not approved private installation**. A public download offer, government host, university affiliation or free electronic reader is not a blanket personal-copy exception. Ask the rights holder when needed. No blanket fair-use claim is made.
- **PDF candidate:** the source format can be submitted individually to the current PDF importer **only if your use is authorized**. Format compatibility does not establish per-document parser acceptance, safety or rights. No catalog entry is represented here as having passed owner import, offline reading/search or two physical USB reboot checks.
- **UNSUPPORTED HTML:** retain authorized originals in their original format outside this importer. FDA HTML/transcript and the illustrated bow-book HTML ZIP are not PDFs, not supported owner imports, and have not been converted into fictional substitute PDFs.

Download/use status and public redistribution status are recorded separately below. Sources with a reusable license remain useful references, but this catalog does not assert whether any particular ISO ships them. Link discovery, source acquisition, byte integrity and parser compatibility are **not safety approval**, technical validation or current professional advice. Historical manuals and local/regional assumptions require particular care.

Evidence reviewed here comes from retained publisher pages, exact-work notices and acquisition records dated 2026-11-05. Links were reconciled to those records without re-downloading the manuals; the Car Care Council landing link was also read back online and redirects to the URL noted in its entry. This is not a guarantee of future availability or unchanged terms. A direct file is labeled as such, never substituted for a missing verified landing page.
