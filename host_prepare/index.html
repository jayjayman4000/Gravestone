Prepare your private Gravestone pack

On your normal computer, before any USB work. This local tool does not download manuals, flash drives, mount disks or modify your Gravestone installation.

Private, not encrypted. Do not include passwords, identification, addresses or medical secrets. Keep the resulting ZIP out of public Git, cloud shares and distributed images. Closing or refreshing this page discards unsaved entries; no automatic draft storage.

1. Record equipment

Use exact models, record unknowns explicitly. No model is selected for you. Official links are records only: acquire any permitted originals yourself in your normal browser.

2. Choose local original PDFs

Optional: up to 16 files, 32 MiB each, 64 MiB total. Originals remain unchanged. No PDF parser, OCR, safety review or extraction runs here; malformed/encrypted files may be rejected later by the kiosk.

3. Save private pack

4. Transfer deliberately

Save the ZIP to your own private folder using your browser's save dialog. Each download has a distinct random name; never confirm overwriting an existing backup. Check that the download actually completed. Extract to a new empty folder, read TRANSFER.md and retain the original ZIP separately.

This is not a bootable image, kiosk backup or bulk-import format. Transfer permitted PDFs individually through the kiosk's Other PDF importer using a separate ordinary data drive. Do not upload this ZIP to PDF or map tools. Inventory remains a private ordinary file, not a kiosk index. Maps use separate supported map tools.

Do not reflash, format or repartition an existing USB to transfer these files. USB persistence must already be provisioned and verified across two clean reboots; this tool cannot provide it. Notebook/map backups exclude personal PDFs. Keep another private backup.
